Numeral System

The numeral system is a little-endian bijective base 16 system.
Where N is the expressed quantity and d is a digit, N = 160d1 + 161d2 + ... + 16(nd - 1)dnd.
The quantity of a noun can be expressed by making the counted noun be possessive of the numeral (eg., one of an object:  1rAG   /ꜛˈtʰäːʋ ꜛˈr̠äː/  <taau raa>).
Numerical labelling can be expressed by having the numeral as a compounding noun onto the labelled noun (eg., object #1:  raG1   /r̠ə ꜛˈtʰäːʋ/  <ra taau>).

absence, emptiness, lack, zero (n):  flēT  /ꜛˈɸːleð̞/  <flēth>
