KXRh Draconic Script

The script is abugidic.
Differentiation in syllable break location is fully contrastive, structurally and audibly.
The syllable break environments /VC.V/, /VCC.V/, /V.CCV/, and /VCC.CV/ are considered to be irregular, and, in romanization, cause the consonant immediately contacting the boundary to duplicate.
In the irregular syllable break environment /VCC.CV/, the consonant immediately contacting the left edge of the syllable boundary is the only consonant that duplicates in romanization.
Root words that are not proper nouns may have a maximum of 3 syllables.

Consonant Table

Onsets and codas may have a maximum of 2 consonants.
Two of the same consonant being adjacent to each other is illegal.
Non-suffix syllables must have a consonant.
Root words must end in a consonant.
Words ending in <gh> have the <gh> dropped in pronunciation and romanization.
<gh> becomes <h> when postvocalic.
<w> becomes <u> when postvocalic, except when after <u> or <uu>, or when doubled in an irregular syllable break environment — this is romanizational only and does not indicate vocalization.
<y> becomes <i> when postvocalic, except when after <ii> or when doubled in an irregular syllable break environment — this is romanizational only and does not indicate vocalization.

Vowels

/V.V/ is illegal.
Stress and pitch raising are an inherent characteristic of vowel quality and are never generated by any other means.
Pitch-raising only applies to the syllable containing the pitched-up vowel.
Stressed/pitch-raised vowels stress/pitch-raise the entire syllable containing them.
A word may contain multiple primary stresses, none of which become secondary.
<ae>, <ea>, <ee>, <oo>, and <u> can be used in place of <ä>, <ë>, <ē>, <ō> and <'> in environments not supporting the transcription, however, using the alternatives otherwise is wholly improper.
By default, vowels come after the consonant they attach to, however, if a VC symbol comes before the consonant, this order is reversed.
As shown in the chart below, to represent a lone vowel, attach it after a <gh> with a VC symbol.

Punctuation

Word seperation is implicit before a preceding symbol or after a proceeding symbol, and as such, a sentence seperator should not be written in those circumstance.
Romanizational diphthongs only have the first letter capitalized when in an acronym.
Vowels cannot be a base letter in an acronym.
Acronyms can have vowel suffixes attached to them, in which case, the vowel is attached directly to the last consonant (CV).
